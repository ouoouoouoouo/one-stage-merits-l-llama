$\dagger$ Configured as a cosine decay to $0.05$, but the schedule is defined

$\ddagger$ Configured to switch off at 30\% of the budget, i.e.\ epoch~30, but

mean $\pm$ std over 5 seeds; \emph{best} is the maximum over those seeds and is
reported for comparability with prior work, not as a result.
